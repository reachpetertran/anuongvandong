*The site is written for Vietnamese readers; the Vietnamese version is the original. Quotes from Vietnamese documents are unofficial translations.*

This page lists only numbers checked on a government website. Checked on 24 November 2026. These numbers are for **Vietnam**.

## Medical emergency: call 113

- **113** takes requests for medical emergencies. A Ministry of Health circular, "Regulation on receiving information and the pre-hospital emergency procedure", says every request for medical help made to 113 is routed to health staff on duty at the 113 call centre. [Verified]
- Source: the digitally signed copy on the Government portal: <https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/11/42-byt.signed.pdf>
- Number 42/2026/TT-BYT, signed 31 Oct 2026, in force 1 Nov 2026: according to the press; the PDF we viewed leaves the number and date fields blank. Being checked.

When you call, say: the address and a landmark, what happened, whether the person is awake and breathing, and your phone number. Stay on the line for instructions. [Inference]

## Child protection: call 111

- **111** – National child protection hotline, free, 24/7. [Verified]
- Source: <https://cuctreem.molisa.gov.vn/>

## Feedback on health services

- **1900 9095** – Ministry of Health hotline. For **complaints and feedback** about health services. It is **not** a mental-health support line. [Verified]
- Source: <https://moh.gov.vn/en/danh-ba-uong-day-nong/-/asset_publisher/OYL33cPG6EH4/content/-uong-day-nong-bo-y-te?inheritRedirect=false>

## If you feel overwhelmed, or are thinking about harming yourself

- Tell someone you trust right now.
- Go to the nearest health facility. If there is immediate danger, call **113**.
- A national mental-health line: the site has **not yet verified** one on a government website, so none is listed.
