*The site is written for Vietnamese readers; the Vietnamese version is the original.*

## In short

- **No cookies.** No log-in. No forms.
- **No tracking tools**, no visitor analytics, no ads.
- **No collection of your health information** in any form.

## Hosting

The site is a static site hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web server, the infrastructure provider may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run the service and block attacks. The site does not use these logs to identify or track readers. [Inference]

## Outbound links

Articles link to WHO, Vietnam's Ministry of Health, foreign health agencies and scientific journals. When you follow a link, that site's privacy policy applies.
