Sources and how we edit

The site is written for Vietnamese readers; the Vietnamese version is the original.

Order of sources

  1. WHO.
  2. Vietnam's Ministry of Health and National Institute of Nutrition.
  3. Official guidelines from the US (DGA, PAG, CDC), Australia (Department of Health, NHMRC) and the UK (NHS, SACN).
  4. Professional bodies (AASM, AAP…).
  5. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses in peer-reviewed journals.

The site does not use blogs, forums, shops or online influencers as sources.

Editing rules

  • Every figure carries source – version/year – check date.
  • A figure not yet checked against the original is not published.
  • Observational results are written as "associated with", not as cause and effect.
  • Every article has "Who should take care" and "When to see a health worker" sections.
  • Articles on weight, fasting or dieting give no personal calorie or weight targets and never shame bodies.
  • Every article has a Vietnamese version (the original) and an English version with the same check date.

Update schedule

  • Every article has a re-check date, usually 6 months after the check date.
  • When an official guideline changes, the related articles are corrected and re-dated.

Author and tools

  • Articles are drafted with the help of AI tools. Every figure is checked against its original source before publication.
