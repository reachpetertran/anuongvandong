The site is written for Vietnamese readers; the Vietnamese version is the original. Quotes from Vietnamese documents are unofficial translations.

The emergency, child-protection and health-feedback numbers were checked against government documents or websites. The "Ngày Mai" line is a community project, not a government line; the author decided to list it on 26 November 2026. Checked on 26 November 2026. These numbers are for Vietnam.

Medical emergency: call 113

  • 113 takes requests for medical emergencies. A Ministry of Health circular, "Regulation on receiving information and the pre-hospital emergency procedure", says every request for medical help made to 113 is routed to health staff on duty at the 113 call centre. Verified
  • 115 still works during the transition to the 113 call centre. In Ho Chi Minh City, for example, calls to 114 and 115 are routed automatically to 113 (Đại biểu Nhân dân newspaper, 3 Nov 2026). Verified
  • Sources: Circular 42/2026/TT-BYT of 31 Oct 2026, in force 1 Nov 2026, Article 2(1) (digitally signed copy on the Government portal): https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/11/42-byt.signed.pdf; Đại biểu Nhân dân: https://daibieunhandan.vn/tp-ho-chi-minh-tich-hop-tong-dai-khan-cap-113-114-115-thanh-tong-dai-113-10441635.html

When you call, say: the address and a landmark, what happened, whether the person is awake and breathing, and your phone number. Stay on the line for instructions. Inference

Child protection: call 111

  • 111 – National child protection hotline, free, 24/7. Verified
  • Source: https://cuctreem.molisa.gov.vn/

Feedback on health services

  • 1900 9095 – Ministry of Health hotline. For complaints and feedback about health services. It is not a mental-health support line. Verified
  • Source: https://moh.gov.vn/en/danh-ba-uong-day-nong/-/asset_publisher/OYL33cPG6EH4/content/-uong-day-nong-bo-y-te?inheritRedirect=false

If you feel overwhelmed, or are thinking about harming yourself

  • Tell someone you trust right now.
  • Go to the nearest health facility. If there is immediate danger, call 113.
  • 096 306 1414 – Ngày Mai hotline ("Tomorrow"). A non-profit community project giving free emotional support by phone to people in crisis, especially people with depression, and their carers. Hours according to Befrienders Worldwide: Wednesday to Sunday, 13:00–20:30; closed Monday and Tuesday. It is not an emergency line. Outside these hours or if there is immediate danger: call 113. [Number and hours verified on Befrienders Worldwide; not on a government website]
  • Sources: Befrienders Worldwide: https://befrienders.org/find-support-now/hotlinen-gaymai/; Find A Helpline: https://findahelpline.com/organizations/ngay-mai
  • A government national mental-health line: not yet verified by the site.
